How occasion pages work

Your team creates one WordPress page per SEO topic (e.g. “Wedding Car Rental Dubai”). In the editor you set the occasion, hero image, which vehicles appear (by tag or manual pick), FAQ, and body copy. Visitors never see an “occasion” filter on that page — the topic is already fixed in the heading.

This hub page lists all occasions; individual listing pages drive Google traffic for long-tail keywords.
